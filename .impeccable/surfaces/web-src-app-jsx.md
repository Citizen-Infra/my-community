# Philanthropic XXI deployment identity

**Mode:** Operate. A member opening the dedicated Philanthropic XXI deployment should immediately recognize the group while retaining the familiar dashboard tasks and states.

**Scope:** Apply a fixed deployment identity only when `VITE_PINNED_COMMUNITY_ID=philanthropic-xxi`. Replace top-level My Community naming, metadata, install identity, and design tokens across the dashboard and decision routes. Preserve feed structure, authentication, settings, navigation, responsive behavior, civic-state semantics, and every ordinary My Community deployment. The existing light/dark/system preference remains available through a new dark companion to the established PXXI world.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The PXXI member dashboard is a working field survey, not a generic white-label portal; deployment identity changes the material and voice without changing the instrument panel underneath.

**OWN-WORLD:** The brain repo's geological survey sheet is authoritative: buff-grey ground, green-black ink, cobalt links and focus, oxblood emphasis, flat rules, Archivo structure, Archivo Narrow labels, Literata reading text, and the four-bar confidence mark. Dark mode becomes a restrained night survey with mineral-pale ink and contrast-adjusted cobalt and oxblood, never neon, glow, gradients, or the default My Community green.

**STORY:** Arrive in Philanthropic XXI, scan the group's live signals, open a contribution or decision, and move through member-only material without crossing into a visually different product.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** “Philanthropic XXI” and the confidence-bar mark anchor the top bar on survey-paper ground; the existing dashboard mosaic begins immediately below in the same flat, ruled material. Status and provenance remain legible, controls remain familiar, and the identity persists through loading and focused routes.

**FORM:** User-pinned PXXI geological survey sheet; direction seed `user-pinned-pxxi-survey-sheet` (no concept roll because the existing brain-repo identity is the approved authority).
